# Swapnil AI Labs — Intelligence for everyone
Mission: global open-source AI research, infrastructure and applications useful to technical and non-technical people. Reuse shared security, model, evaluation, context and memory layers; expose task-focused interfaces instead of assuming users know APIs.
## Operating model
Ten pillars: everyday AI, professional AI, industry AI, education, developers, research, languages, social benefit/accessibility, infrastructure, community. The machine-readable catalog has 24 industries, 72 proposed applications, 12 research tracks and 22 candidate language/locale entries. All products are explicitly planned; the earlier authenticated multi-domain reference service is separately implemented and tested. No consumer products or multilingual model quality are implied.
## Release sequencing
Wave 1: document explanation, study practice, small-business invoice/FAQ assistance, and developer agent evaluation. Synthetic or openly licensed records only; ship read-only and advisory experiences. Wave 2: retail, operations, agriculture and logistics design partners; validate workflow outcomes and offline/low-bandwidth usability. Wave 3: regulated domains only after local professional review, consent, legal assessment, independent security tests and production controls. Wave 4: scale multilingual interfaces and licensed domain datasets based on community demand, not on arbitrary language counts.
## Accessibility and languages
Start with authored English, Hindi and Hinglish interface copy. Native-speaker review, locale formatting, keyboard and screen reader testing, RTL support for applicable scripts, code-switching, low-bandwidth pages, text-first accessibility and quality benchmarks precede language support claims. No voice or model translation capability is established by the catalog.
## Shared data and safety
Every application has a data card (origin, rights, consent, retention, deletion, PII handling), acceptance dataset, safety policy, human escalation, evidence registry and measured quality. High-risk categories are advisory/read-only with mandatory human review; prohibit automated diagnosis, trading, hiring decisions, legal outcomes, benefit determinations and critical infrastructure control. Local regulations and professional review apply. No customer data in public source.
## Business and partnerships
Open-source core and catalog; free educational resources where sustainable; paid implementation, managed hosting only after production gates, industry pilots and research collaborations. Start with teachers, small-business operators, automation agencies, accessible-tech groups and sector-specific domain experts as design partners. Use public verified contacts; seek real workflow feedback and reproducible evaluation, not generic promotion. No claimed partner or user unless verified.
## Maintainability
Use a versioned registry, issue-to-evidence trace, per-product owners and stage transitions: planned → prototype → validated synthetic → independent evaluation → controlled pilot → production accepted. Never promote a stage without evidence and human approval. Netlify remains deferred. Repository rename remains a settings operation.
