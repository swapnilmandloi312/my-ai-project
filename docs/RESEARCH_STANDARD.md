# Swapnil AI Labs research and evidence standard
Every experiment has an ID, a falsifiable hypothesis, source URLs and access dates, frozen data and code revision, declared baseline, evaluation protocol, resource budget, results including failures, known leakage/selection bias, and a claim boundary. Separate authored fixtures, independent holdouts, and live customer data. Never describe a passing implementation test as a scientific discovery.
Record model identifier and revision, decoding settings, tokenizer and dataset versions, environment and dependency versions, seed, raw responses or privacy-safe derivatives, costs and latency where measured. Never infer real cost from hypothetical routing units. Do not reuse evaluation cases as training data. Obtain permission/license before collecting or distributing datasets.
Acceptance tiers: T0 proposal; T1 runnable prototype; T2 verified synthetic tests; T3 independent holdout/strong baseline; T4 scoped customer pilot; T5 production acceptance. A component cannot claim a higher tier without evidence. Research results may be negative.
Before public publication review prior art, disclosure of potential vulnerabilities, third-party notices and founder's licensing/IP strategy. Public repository visibility does not grant an open-source license.
