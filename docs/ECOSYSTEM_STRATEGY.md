# Swapnil AI Labs — long-term open-source ecosystem
Mission: build trustworthy, reusable infrastructure for intelligent systems, from India for global developers. Lead with execution reliability and reproducible research, not generic AI consulting or unsupported novelty claims.
## Product layers
Core: contracts, compiler, controlled execution, persistent authorization. Intelligence: context, memory, routing, caching and graph. Engineering: evaluation, diagnostics, telemetry, reproducibility and SDKs. Experimental protocols: tenant-scoped message exchange and offline candidate acceptance. The current system is a local synthetic research platform; modules must pass independent validation before production adoption.
## Adoption and company positioning
Primary audience: AI agent/tooling startups, automation platform teams, FDEs and AI infrastructure engineers. Secondary: researchers and enterprise design partners. Position as a complementary control/evaluation layer, not a replacement for model providers, authorization providers or orchestration frameworks.
Contribute useful sample integrations, reproducible bug reports on authorized test environments, benchmark corrections and documentation. Ask for scoped design feedback, not generic sponsorship. Publish negative results and maintain issue-to-evidence links.
## Partnerships
Priority A: tool/authentication platforms and orchestration SDKs (integration examples). Priority B: model and evaluation platforms (same-workload benchmarks). Priority C: universities and developer communities (independent labels, reproducibility). Priority D: design partners (non-sensitive read-only pilots, agreed success criteria).
Record source of contact, prior contact history, company-specific technical relevance, exact proposed artifact, evidence links, sent date, reply and next action. Avoid duplicate messages and unsupported security claims. Honor opt-outs. Never use confidential client data or imply affiliation.
## Open-source governance
MIT for original project materials whose rights are held by contributors; dependencies retain their own terms. Research datasets and models require separate rights assessment. Issues and PRs are public; credentials, proprietary third-party data and personal information are not. Public release does not imply production safety.
## Sustainable business
Open-source core, paid implementation and scoped reliability audits, optional managed hosting after security/operations acceptance, and research partnerships. Pricing is an experiment; validate demand before building billing. Track active external contributors, reproducible reports, integration usage, design partner conversations, pilot outcomes, issue resolution and revenue; no fabricated metrics.
## Gates
0: reproducible local platform; 1: independent holdout and design partner feedback; 2: production identity, document ACLs, provider budgets, audit integrity and incident response; 3: licensed model/data research and measured inference; 4: managed service and global distribution. Netlify is explicitly deferred. New foundation models, general-purpose compiler and AI operating system remain research programs, not completed products.
