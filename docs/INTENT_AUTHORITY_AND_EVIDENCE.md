# Explicit intent authority and evidence integrity — local candidate
A trusted human/host issuer independently authorizes a read and creates a short-lived HMAC grant binding principal, tenant, exact request, target, intent MAC, audience, purpose and nonce. The wrapper verifies and atomically consumes the grant before any runtime inference. Revocation before admission and replay are checked; downstream runtime still applies its own scope, quotas, budgets and proposal validation. Revocation after admission cannot cancel ongoing inference. Issuing a grant does not validate actual human consent: the issuer must do so independently. Direct legacy runtime/HTTP paths are still present; production must route exclusively through an authenticated broker and eliminate bypasses. Not yet wired into the goal-agent evaluator or Work Relay, no external write permissions. No cryptographic claim beyond local HMAC with trusted keys.

Evidence manifest binds tenant, reference, source label, version, length and content digest. Verification detects mismatches against an independently trusted manifest; does not authenticate the original source, verify semantic claims or enforce immutability in storage. Detached HMAC audit checkpoint detects truncation before a recorded head and allows valid append-only extension when held externally. The primitive is implemented; no external storage/account, independent anchor service or production audit has been configured. Both keys require independent secret storage; untrusted clients and models must never receive them. Tests use synthetic data and deterministic provider, not external evaluation.
